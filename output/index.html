Notes

  • Source: Event-Core tables (event.txt) of each Darwin-Core Archive (31 program directories). Occurrence totals per dataset come from the eventID → occurrence.txt linkage.
  • For the map and time series, decimalLatitude/decimalLongitude and year of all events were pre-aggregated with Python (h3 4.5, pandas) into H3 hexagons (resolutions 5 & 7) and per-year counts; no individual records are shipped with this page.
  • 3014, 3019, 4045 and 566 intentionally carry no event coordinates (polygon mapping sources without centroids) — they appear in the table and time series but are not mappable.
  • 911 (NPS seagrass monitoring) contains zero events and zero occurrences.
  • Dataset 71 (National Wetland Inventory) contributes ≈1.04M events state-wide — the coarse res-5 view keeps it legible; zoom in for its fine-grained (res-7) footprint.
  • Seagrass filter: an event counts as seagrass/SAV when its Event-Core habitat is Submerged Aquatic Vegetation or contains “seagrass”. In Event-Core these events carry habitat = Submerged Aquatic Vegetation; the filter drops water-quality (Water Column, Riverine…), wetland (Estuarine and Marine Wetland…) and oyster/coral events. 25 of 31 program directories report SAV events; the earliest are the polygon mapping programs, with dense monitoring coverage from the mid-1990s onwards.
